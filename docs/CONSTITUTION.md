# Budget Constitution v0.1

The Constitution is Level 0 policy. Learned routing, Board precedent, and runtime optimization may not override it.

1. **No silent overspend.** Every paid or bounded resource must be admitted, reserved, and settled.
2. **No hidden quality-floor violation.** A route below the mandate's quality floor is disqualified, not "degraded."
3. **No authority expansion for economic convenience.** Optimization cannot grant capabilities.
4. **No evidence-floor violation for decisive claims.**
5. **No semantic reuse across incompatible trust, purpose, freshness, or authority scopes.**
6. **No cache substitution for a required external side effect.**
7. **No savings claim without an explicit, auditable baseline.**
8. **No destructive or lossy transform without retrievable provenance when provenance is required.**
9. **No uncontrolled recursive escalation.** Escalation must be bounded and funded.
10. **A cheaper route that cannot be adequately verified is not necessarily cheaper.**
11. **Human attention is a scarce resource and must not be used as a free fallback.**
12. **Failure and negative results remain visible to audit and learning.**
13. **Learned policy cannot modify the Constitution.**
14. **Provider/model self-reported confidence is not empirical qualification.**
15. **A cached answer is never proof that a requested mutation occurred.**

## Policy levels

- **Level 0 — Constitution:** operator-controlled invariants.
- **Level 1 — Board policy:** budgets, portfolios, risk posture, model/provider qualification.
- **Level 2 — Operating policy:** thresholds, route mappings, cache TTLs, queue thresholds.

BudgetPass enforces the compiled result. It does not reinterpret Level 0 policy.
