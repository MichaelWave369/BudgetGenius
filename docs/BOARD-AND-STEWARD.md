# Board and Steward

The Board exists above BudgetGenius because "what is the cheapest qualified route?" and "are these the right rules for this situation?" are different questions.

## Logical seats

- Treasury: capital, reserves, liquidity, quotas.
- Systems: runtime capability, congestion, latency, reliability.
- Science: empirical quality and uncertainty.
- Risk: consequence of failure, rollback, security.
- Evidence: provenance, source fidelity, quality debt.
- Memory: reuse, freshness, NBG lineage.
- Operator Advocate: operator intent and interruption cost.
- Auditor: whether claimed savings and quality are real.

These are logical responsibilities, not a requirement to spend eight LLM calls per request.

## Steward

The Steward is the specialized synthesizer. It does not get universal authority. It resolves soft tradeoffs only after hard constraints eliminate invalid routes.

The Board should usually compile policy snapshots. BudgetPass executes those snapshots without convening a deliberation for ordinary calls.

## Exception triggers

Board/Steward review becomes appropriate when:

- no route meets the mandate,
- projected spend crosses a policy threshold,
- contingency/emergency funds are needed,
- a novel task class appears,
- routing regret or quality debt spikes,
- model/provider qualification changes,
- high-risk or irreversible actions are proposed,
- a large multi-agent campaign needs a Cognitive Grant,
- policy conflicts,
- the operator explicitly requests review.

## Ruling outputs

An exceptional decision may produce:

1. **Ruling** — what happens now.
2. **Precedent** — whether similar future cases may reuse the ruling.
3. **Policy candidate** — whether repeated successful precedent should become a deterministic fast path.

Minority/dissent records should be retained so later failures can be compared with objections that existed before execution.
